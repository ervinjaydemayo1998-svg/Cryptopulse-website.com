Why Bitcoin Dominance Matters in 2025

Bitcoin dominance is the percentage of total crypto market cap controlled by Bitcoin, and in 2025 it matters more than ever. When dominance rises, it signals investors moving to “safer” crypto assets. When it drops, altcoins usually explode in value. Traders use this metric to identify rotation cycles, avoid bad entries, and predict market sentiment. If Bitcoin dominance rises above 55%, expect altcoins to slow; if it falls below 45%, prepare for a potential altcoin season.
